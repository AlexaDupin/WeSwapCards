We’re about to release the WeSwapCards Android app! And we’re looking for about 15 collectors to test it for two weeks.

It’s easy: install it and use it now and then. No minimum time, no report to write. We will value your feedback, but it is not mandatory.

You’ll sign in with your existing WeSwapCards account and will be able to manage your swaps from the app, with notifications when someone replies!

To join, reply to this email with the email address of the Google account you use on your Android phone. Google Play needs it to give you access.

Android only for now. iPhone version is in progress.
